Questions

Where do the numbers come from?

From the nutrition guide each restaurant publishes, and every menu shows the date we last checked it. When a custom order has a part the restaurant doesn't list on its own, we use the USDA food database and mark that part as an estimate. Nothing is guessed by AI.

What is a cheat code?

A custom order built from the regular menu, like a burger in a lettuce wrap with an extra patty. The app gives you the exact words to say and the calories, protein, carbs and fat for the finished order.

Which restaurants are in the app?

43 chains today, fast food and sit-down, from McDonald's and Chick-fil-A to Chili's and Texas Roadhouse. More are added as we check their numbers.

Is it free?

It is free while it is in beta on TestFlight.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. CheatCode is on iPhone only for now.
