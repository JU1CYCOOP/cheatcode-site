Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · North Cove Wellness LLC

The short version

CheatCode has no accounts and no ads. What you enter stays on your phone. North Cove Wellness LLC does not collect it, sell it or share it.

What the app stores on your phone

Your goal, sex, age, height, weight, goal weight, activity level, diet type, foods you like and dislike, meal numbers, favorites, saved orders and imported recipes. This is saved in the app's storage on your device so the app can work. It is not sent to North Cove Wellness LLC. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to show restaurants near you and how far they are. The search runs through Apple Maps on your device. The restaurants it finds are remembered on your phone so the list opens quickly; where you were is not saved. North Cove Wellness LLC does not receive or keep your location. You can turn it off in iPhone Settings and the app still works.

Apple Health

If you tap "Use my weight from Apple Health" in setup, the app reads your latest body weight, in setup and when it checks your weight every two weeks, to keep its guess at your calories and protein right. It reads nothing else and writes nothing. Your Health data stays on your phone. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone. You can turn it off in the Health app at any time.

Links you paste

When you import a recipe from a link, your phone loads that page directly from the site it belongs to (for example TikTok, Instagram or YouTube) to read the caption. That site sees the request the same way it would if you opened the link in a browser.

Menu updates

The app may download newer menu and nutrition data. That request carries no information about you.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through Apple. North Cove Wellness LLC never sees your card details. Your phone asks Apple whether you have a subscription. North Cove Wellness LLC is not told who subscribed, only sales totals from Apple.

Email

If you write to us, we get your email address and whatever you send, and we use it only to answer you.

Analytics and tracking

None. The app has no advertising, no third-party analytics and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

CheatCode is not made for children under 13 and is not meant to be used by them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
