Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026 · North Cove Wellness LLC

1. Summary of Data Practices

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes the practices of North Cove Wellness LLC (the "Company") with respect to information in connection with the CheatCode mobile application (the "Application") and the Company's website (together with the Application, the "Services"). Each individual who downloads, accesses or uses the Services is referred to in this Policy as a "User."

The Application does not offer or require user accounts, does not display advertising, does not contain third-party analytics or tracking technology, and does not track Users. Information that a User enters into the Application is stored locally on the User's device and is not collected, accessed, sold or shared by the Company. The only personal information the Company receives is information that a User voluntarily sends to the Company by email.

This Policy sets forth what the Application does with information, which information is handled by third parties such as Apple Inc. ("Apple"), and the choices and rights available to Users.

2. Identity of the Controller

The Services are provided by North Cove Wellness LLC. To the extent the Company processes personal information, which is limited principally to correspondence that Users send to the Company, the Company is the party responsible for that information and acts as the "controller" within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR and comparable laws. The Company may be contacted at thecheatcodeteam@gmail.com.

3. Information Collected by the Company

The Company does not collect any information through the Application. The Application does not transmit User information to any server operated by or on behalf of the Company. Without limiting the foregoing, the Company does not collect any User's name, email address, telephone number, location, health data, body measurements, food choices, contacts, photographs, device identifiers, advertising identifier or usage data. Accordingly, the App Store privacy label for the Application states "Data Not Collected."

The Company receives personal information only when a User contacts the Company directly, as described in "Correspondence with the Company." Apple may also furnish the Company with limited reports that do not identify any User, as described in "Information Received from Apple."

4. Information Stored on the User's Device

In order to function, the Application stores the following categories of information in its own storage on the User's device:

  • Onboarding responses: the User's goal, how often the User eats out, what the User finds most difficult, how the User prefers to choose food, how the User learned of CheatCode, and any referral code the User entered.
  • Physical details: sex, age, height, weight, goal weight, the User's preferred rate of progress and activity level.
  • Dietary settings: diet type, foods the User likes and dislikes, meals per day, and the User's calorie, protein, carbohydrate and fat targets.
  • Restaurants and saved items: the restaurants the User selected, the User's favorites and any recipes the User imported.
  • Nearby restaurants: restaurant locations found during the most recent search (names, map positions, distances and drive times), retained so that the list opens promptly. The User's own location is not saved.
  • Application settings: preferences such as appearance, sort order, which tips the User has seen, whether Apple Health access is enabled and the date of the User's most recent weight check.
  • A prepared copy of the menu data, retained so that the Application opens promptly.

Such information is processed solely on the User's device, for the purposes of calculating the User's suggested meal size, ranking orders by their fit to the User's targets and retaining the User's choices. It is not transmitted to the Company or to any other party. The Company cannot access, modify or recover such information.

If a User backs up the device to iCloud or to a computer, the backup may include Application data. Any such backup is administered by Apple or by the User's computer, subject to Apple's terms, and is not accessible to the Company.

5. Location Information

Use of location is optional. If the User grants permission, and only while the User is using the Application, the Application uses the device's location to identify the restaurants nearest to the User and to display their distance. The Application requests location accurate to approximately 100 meters, which is sufficient for that purpose. If the User declines, the Application continues to function without distances.

The User's device determines the User's location. To find restaurants, the Application queries Apple Maps, through Apple's built-in frameworks, for each restaurant's locations in the area surrounding the User, and for drive times to the nearest few. Those queries include the area surrounding the User and are sent to Apple, not to the Company. Apple processes them under its own privacy policy. The map on the Home screen and the place cards (hours, telephone number and directions) are likewise provided by Apple Maps.

Distances are calculated on the User's device. The Application stores the restaurants it found, not the User's location. The Company never receives, stores or shares any User's location. The User may disable location access at any time in the device's Settings for CheatCode.

6. Apple Health (HealthKit) Data

Use of Apple Health is optional and read-only. Only if the User selects "Use my weight from Apple Health" during setup and grants access on Apple's permission screen does the Application read the User's most recent body weight and the date on which it was recorded. The Application reads this information during setup and when it checks the User's weight every two weeks, in order to keep the User's suggested calories and protein current.

The Application reads no other Health data and never writes any data to Health. The weight it reads is stored only in the Application's storage on the User's device, in the same manner as a weight the User enters manually. The Application never transmits Health data to any party, including the Company and iCloud. Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and is never sold, rented or shared with any party.

The User may revoke this access at any time in the privacy settings of the Health app. The Application does not have a separate Health setting.

7. Recipe Import from Links

If a User imports a recipe from a link, the User's device connects directly to the website concerned (for example, TikTok, Instagram or YouTube) and, in the case of TikTok and YouTube, to their public link preview services, in order to retrieve the post's public caption, author name and cover image. Any text appearing on the cover image is read on the User's device. The website receives the request substantially as it would if the User opened the link in a browser, including the User's internet protocol (IP) address, and handles it under its own privacy policy. No information about the request is transmitted to the Company.

The Application can detect that the User has copied a link, without reading its contents, in order to offer "Import the link you copied." The Application reads the copied content only when the User selects that option or Paste, and iOS may request the User's permission first.

Imported recipes, including the link, caption and cover image address, are stored only on the User's device. When the User opens an imported recipe, its cover image loads from the website from which it was imported.

8. Menu and Nutrition Data Updates

The Application is distributed with its menu data built in. From time to time the Application may download newer menu and nutrition data from a file host. Such requests contain no information about the User. As with any internet request, the host receives the User's IP address and basic technical details, which the host may retain in its ordinary server logs. The Company does not use such information to identify any person.

9. Purchases and Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. The Company never receives any User's name, Apple Account email address, card or payment details, or billing address. On the User's device, the Application asks Apple whether the User's Apple Account has an active subscription or free trial and verifies Apple's signed purchase records. Apple processes each purchase under its own privacy policy and the App Store terms.

Apple provides the Company with aggregate sales and subscription reports, such as the number of subscriptions or renewals in each country. Such reports do not identify any User.

10. Information Received from Apple

If a User has enabled "Share With App Developers" in the device's analytics settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with the Company in a form that does not identify the User. The Company uses such information solely to correct problems and improve the Application. The User may disable this sharing at any time under Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements. Apple also makes available to the Company aggregate App Store figures, such as the number of product page views and downloads, which do not identify any person.

11. Correspondence with the Company

If a User emails the Company, including through Contact us in the Application's Settings or to request a restaurant, the Company receives the User's email address, the User's name if it appears in the email, the content of the message and any attachments. The Company uses such information solely to read and respond to the message, to correct problems the User reports and to maintain records of its support. The Company does not add Users to any mailing list or use their email addresses for advertising.

The Company's email provider stores messages on its servers on the Company's behalf. The Company retains support correspondence only for as long as necessary to assist the User and for its records, and deletes it earlier upon request, unless the law requires its retention.

12. Sharing Features and Third-Party Links

If a User selects the Share button, the Application creates an image of an order on the User's device and passes it to the iOS share sheet. The destination is solely the User's choice, and each recipient handles the image under its own terms. Links in the Application, such as a restaurant's nutrition source, open the website concerned, which is governed by its own privacy policy.

13. No Advertising, Tracking or Sale of Personal Information

The Company represents the following with respect to the Application:

  • The Application contains no advertising and no advertising technology.
  • The Application contains no third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • The Application does not track Users across applications or websites owned by other companies, and therefore never requests permission to track.
  • The Company does not sell, rent, trade or share personal information, including for targeted advertising, and has not done so.
  • The Company does not make automated decisions about any User that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Suggested targets are calculated on the User's device, and the User may change them.

Because the Company does not track Users or sell or share their information, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals have no further practice to disable.

14. Children's Privacy

The Services are not designed for or directed to children under the age of 13. The Company does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under 16 where applicable law sets a higher age, and the Application itself collects no information from any person. If any person believes that a child has sent personal information to the Company by email, that person should contact the Company, and the Company will delete it.

Minors should use the Application only with the permission of a parent or legal guardian and should consult a physician before changing their diet.

15. Retention and Deletion

Information stored on the User's device remains there until the User deletes it. The User may change any setup response in Settings at any time, and Replay onboarding in Settings clears the User's setup responses and restarts setup. Deleting the Application deletes all information it stored on the device. Copies contained in device backups are removed as those backups are replaced, in accordance with Apple's practices.

The Company holds only correspondence that Users send to it. A User may request deletion of such correspondence at thecheatcodeteam@gmail.com.

Deleting the Application does not cancel a subscription. To cancel, the User must open Settings, Apple Account, Subscriptions.

16. Data Security

Information stored by the Application is kept in the Application's private storage area on the User's device, which iOS protects with encryption when the device has a passcode. The Application's internet requests use secure, encrypted connections. Users are advised to protect their devices with a passcode. No device, network or service is perfectly secure, and to the fullest extent permitted by law, the Company is not responsible for any unauthorized access to a User's device or data by any third party.

17. Privacy Rights of Users

Because the Application collects no information, the Company holds no Application data relating to any User. Each User controls all such data on the User's device: the User may view and change it in the Application and delete it by deleting the Application.

With respect to correspondence that a User sends to the Company, and depending on the User's place of residence (for example, under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, other U.S. state privacy laws, Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados or Australia's Privacy Act 1988), the User may have the right to:

  • know what personal information the Company holds about the User and obtain a copy;
  • correct such information;
  • delete such information;
  • restrict or object to its processing;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw any consent previously given;
  • opt out of any sale, sharing, targeted advertising or profiling (none of which the Company engages in);
  • be free from discrimination for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, the User should email thecheatcodeteam@gmail.com. The Company may need to verify that a request originates from the User, for example by replying to the same email address. Where the law permits, an authorized agent may submit a request on the User's behalf. The Company will respond within the period required by law, such as one month under the GDPR or 45 days under California law. If the Company declines a request, the User may appeal by replying to the Company's response. The User may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority where the User resides.

18. Legal Bases for Processing (EU and UK)

Where the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies, the Company processes correspondence that a User sends to it because such processing is necessary to respond to the User and to take steps at the User's request, and on the basis of the Company's legitimate interest in providing support and maintaining records. Data that the Application stores only on the User's device remains under the User's control and is not received by the Company. The Application reads the User's weight from Apple Health only with the User's consent, which the User may withdraw at any time.

19. Notice to Residents of California and Other U.S. States

In the preceding 12 months, the only personal information the Company has collected is information that individuals chose to send to it by email, namely identifiers such as an email address and the contents of their messages. The Company obtained such information directly from those individuals and used it solely to respond to them and to provide support. The Company has not sold or shared personal information, used it for targeted advertising, or used sensitive personal information for any purpose that the law permits a consumer to limit. The Company does not knowingly sell or share the personal information of any person under 16. The Company does not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes.

20. International Users

The Company is based in the United States. The Application stores data on the User's device wherever the User is located. If a User emails the Company from outside the United States, the message is processed in the United States and wherever the Company's email provider operates, where privacy laws may differ from those of the User's country of residence. Such transfer is necessary in order to respond to the User.

21. Changes to This Policy

The Company may amend this Policy from time to time. The amended Policy will be published in the Application and on the Company's website with a revised effective date. If the Company ever modifies the Application to collect personal information, the Company will first update this Policy and the App Store privacy details, notify Users in the Application, and obtain consent where the law requires it.
