Where a restaurant does not publish a number for part of an order (for example an extra topping, a sauce, a pump of syrup, a different milk or leaving off a bun), the app uses an estimate: a USDA FoodData Central figure with a portion size CheatCode assumes, or a figure worked out from the restaurant's own numbers.
