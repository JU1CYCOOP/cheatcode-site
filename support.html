Support

Contact us

Email pocketera@gmail.com. In the app, go to Settings, Contact us. Questions, a wrong number, a restaurant you want added or a problem with the app are all welcome.

To help us fix a problem quickly, tell us the restaurant and item if it is about a number, and your iPhone model and iOS version if something is not working. A screenshot helps. We read every message and reply as soon as we can.

Plans and free trial

CheatCode has two plans: Yearly at $29.99 a year with 3 days free for new subscribers, and Monthly at $9.99 a month. These are US prices; Apple shows the price in your country before you buy. Plans renew automatically until you cancel. To avoid being charged when the free days end, cancel at least 24 hours before they end.

Cancel or change your plan

Open Settings, Apple Account, Subscriptions on your iPhone, or in CheatCode go to Settings, Account, Manage or cancel subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the plan renews. Deleting the app does not cancel your plan. After you cancel, you keep access until the end of the time you paid for.

Restore your plan

On a new or reset iPhone, sign in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with, open CheatCode and tap Restore on the plans screen. If a family organizer has to approve a purchase, the plan starts by itself once it is approved.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds, so we cannot give refunds ourselves. To ask Apple for one, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where the numbers come from

From each restaurant's own published nutrition information, as of the date shown in the app. A part a restaurant does not publish is an estimate, usually from USDA FoodData Central, and is marked as one. Restaurants change recipes and portions, so always check with the restaurant if an exact number matters.

A wrong number or a missing restaurant

Email pocketera@gmail.com with the restaurant and the item. We check the restaurant's own information and fix it in an update. To ask for a new restaurant, use Request a restaurant in search, or email us its name.

Nearby restaurants not showing

CheatCode needs location to show the closest restaurants. Turn it on in your iPhone's Settings for CheatCode, under Location, and choose While Using the App. Distances and drive times come from Apple Maps.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional. The app only reads your latest weight, and only if you turned it on in setup. It never writes to Health. To turn it off, use the Health app's privacy settings for apps.

Your data

CheatCode has no accounts and collects nothing. Everything you enter stays on your phone. To start setup again, use Replay onboarding in Settings. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your phone.

Allergies

The app does not show allergen information. Always check ingredients with the restaurant before you order.
