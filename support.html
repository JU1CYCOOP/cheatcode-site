Your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To change or cancel, open Settings, Apple Account, Subscriptions on your iPhone. To get a plan back on a new phone, open CheatCode and tap Restore on the plans screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where the numbers come from

From each restaurant's own published nutrition information, as of the date shown in the app. A part a restaurant does not publish is a USDA estimate and is marked as one.

Allergies

The app does not show allergen information. Always check ingredients with the restaurant.
